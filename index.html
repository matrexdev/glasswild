A living jar on your phone

Glasswild is a game about a sealed jar with a small world inside. Moss, snails, springtails and the animals that follow them live, eat and die by simple rules taken from real ecology. Keep the balance and the jar grows richer; let it slip and it crashes.

From the forest floor to tide pools, caves, dunes and kelp, each jar is a real habitat with real species. Along the way you find rare bugs, raise them in the jar, and send them into bouts on the trail and in the online Arena.

No ads, no tracking. Your jar lives on your phone.
