Support

Help with the game, and how to reach us.

My purchases are missing

Open Profile → Settings → Restore purchases (Profile is the round badge at the top left). The Naturalist Edition and the looks come back on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Amber is kept in your jar's save like everything else you earn, so Restore purchases cannot bring it back on its own.

I have a new phone

Your jar is saved on the phone. To take it along, on the old phone open Profile → Settings → Cloud backup & transfer, back it up and note the 10-character code; on the new phone, enter that code in the same place. If you linked Apple or Google in Settings, signing in with it on the new phone finds your backup too. Setting up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or a computer) also brings the jar along. Your purchases come back with Restore purchases.

Deleting my online data

Open Profile → Settings → Delete online data. Your Arena name, team and fight history, your friends, duels and gifts, your cloud backup and your Apple or Google sign-in are removed from the server at once; your jar on the phone stays. More in the privacy policy.

A purchase went wrong

Purchases are handled by Apple. For a refund or a charge you don't recognise, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact

For a problem, a question or an idea, write to support.glasswild@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened.
