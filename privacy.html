Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version: almost everything stays on your phone. Glasswild has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. If you play in the online Arena, a small anonymous record of your Arena play is kept on our server so that other players can fight your team, and you can delete it in the game at any time.

1. What stays on your phone

Your jar, bugs, gear and progress, your settings, a record of what you have bought, and a log of game events that helps find problems. The game never sends any of this anywhere.

Your phone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include it; Apple handles those under its own terms. Deleting the game deletes all of it from the phone.

2. The online Arena, only if you use it

The Arena opens partway through the game. When you first enter it, the game makes an anonymous account on our server: a random ID, with no e-mail, password, real name or contacts. Kept with that account:

  • your Arena name (two words picked from the game's own lists and a number) and your emblem bug;
  • your defence team: which bugs, their levels and gear, and its strength;
  • your laurels, league, season, wins and losses;
  • the Arena fights you start and the fights against your team: who fought whom, the result, and the taps made during a fight, so it can be replayed;
  • when you were last in the Arena.

Other players see your Arena name, emblem, team, laurels and league. This is used only to run the Arena.

The server is run for us by Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), and handles this data only on our instructions. Like any server, it receives your device's IP address when the game connects. We don't keep the IP address with your Arena record; Supabase may keep it briefly in its own logs.

Deleting it

In the game, open Profile → Settings → Delete online data. Your Arena account, name, team and fight history are removed from the server at once. Your jar on the phone stays.

3. Purchases

Purchases are made and paid through Apple. We never see your card or your Apple Account. The game is told only which item was bought, so that it can give it to you. Restore Purchases asks Apple again.

4. Reminders

If you allow them, reminders are set on your phone itself. No push service and no server is involved.

5. What the game does not do

  • It shows no ads.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites, and never asks for permission to.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

6. Children

The game asks no one for personal information, and the Arena account is anonymous. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

The Arena data is processed to provide the online feature you choose to use. Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK and similar laws you can ask to see, correct or delete it. Deleting is in the game (section 2); for anything else, write to us (section 9).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: write to support.glasswild@gmail.com.
